# Raakh trailer v7: shots to generate

Each shot below has a prompt to paste into ChatGPT (Sora for video,
or image generation where it says *still*). Generate them, name each file
by its **slot** (`w_dhol.mp4`, `o_parcel.png`…) and get them to me (see
*Sending them* at the end). The cut, the score and the grade are mine; any
shot you don't make keeps a stand-in.

## What every shot must be

- **Vertical 9:16**, the highest resolution your plan gives (1080×1920 ideal).
- **Video: 5 seconds.** I use the best 0.5 to 3 seconds of each.
- **No faces.** Hands, backs, feet, silhouettes, people out of focus or cut
  off by the frame. AI faces are what make it look fake.
- **No hands doing anything fiddly** (typing, holding a phone) unless the
  prompt says so. Those are the second thing that looks fake.
- **No text, logos, subtitles or readable screens** unless the prompt spells
  the text out (the parcel and the poster only).
- **No watermark** if your plan allows. If it can't be avoided, tell me which
  corner it's in and I'll frame around it.
- Real-world Delhi, now. Not Bollywood glossy, not a wedding film: this is a
  thriller, so think of it as found footage shot by a good cinematographer.

**Paste this style line at the start of every prompt** (it keeps them
looking like one film):

```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic shot, photorealistic, shot on 35mm film with an anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, natural practical light only, fine film grain, slight handheld movement, moody thriller tone, Delhi, India, present day. No faces visible, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

Colour: the **wedding** is warm, amber and gold, under string lights. **After**
the shot, everything is **cold, blue-teal and dark**, and fire is the only warm
thing in frame. The prompts already ask for this. Keep it if you rewrite one.

## The cut, and where each shot goes

| Time | Section | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Cold open** | Delhi at night in smog. A phone lights up in a dark room: *This phone isn't yours.* The parcel. |
| 0:08 | **The wedding** | On the dhol: lights, the ghodi, the band, a photographer's flash, notes in the air, fireworks, faster and faster. A revolver passes from hand to hand. An arm goes up. **One shot.** |
| 0:21 | **After** | Cold and slow: the empty tent at dawn, a torch in the dark, a jerrycan, a fire on the back plot, ash. *Somebody edited the night.* |
| 0:36 | **The build** | Cuts that get shorter and shorter with timestamps typed over them: a rider pasting posters, an auto in the rain, a door under a tube light, a clock… |
| 0:46 | **Silence** | Black. Nothing. Then a message: **M? Tu hai?** |
| 0:49 | **Seven** | Dawn over Delhi: *Before seven, you'll have to choose.* Then the title. |

**Must have** = the trailer depends on it. **Nice** = makes it richer; skip if short on time.
