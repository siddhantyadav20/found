Raakh: 20 blind players, what we found

Live web build a35254e · 6–8 Nov 2026 · stopped early at your call, so this covers everything logged up to then.

The short version

  • 13 of 20 played to a result. 9 reached an ending: Room 6 seven times, Return to Origin twice. 4 got stuck for good. The other 7 were cut off when we stopped (Kavya and Farhan were in the Episode 3 finale; Priya and Ankit had finished Episode 2; Ira, Manav and Sana were early on).
  • People who finished loved it. Endings rated 8, 8, 8, 8.5, 9, 9, 9, 9 (Dev gave no number). Every finisher wants Chapter 2.
  • One wall breaks the game: the hidden chats in Episode 2. 15 of the 16 who reached Episode 2's second question struggled to open Sameer's locked WhatsApp chats. It took them 15–120 commands, and 3 stopped there for good. The fix is small.
  • A second wall right after it. At the end of Episode 2 the case file says "Nothing else to answer yet", and the episode only ends once you reply to a Nitin message that nothing points you to. 8 players stalled there. Tanvi never got out.
  • Only 2 of the 5 endings were ever reached. Nobody found the Trade, 4:55 or Unopened endings. Nobody noticed "Choose differently" on the end card.

Fix first (ranked by players hit × cost)

1. Hidden chats (Ep2 Q2): the code "pakki" is typed into WhatsApp search, and nothing says so. Confirmed in code (chatLock "pakki", typed into Search).
Hit: Aarav ~50 commands, Ishita STUCK-HARD ~120, Kabir ~45, Vikram ~45, Zoya ~65, Meher ~15, Dev ~35, Tanvi ~60, Rohit STUCK-HARD ~60, Neha ~60, Sahil STUCK-HARD 40+, Kavya ~70, Farhan ~60, Priya ~30, Ankit ~35. Only Rhea got through cleanly.
What they tried: WhatsApp's bottom tabs, filter chips, "+", pull-down and long-press. All of these look tappable and do nothing. Hint 2 names "Nitin's locked chat" but never how to open it. The question also asks "Who wrote 'Dilip ko service gate pe le aao'?", a line you can't have seen yet, so most players got "Nitin" by elimination.
The route that worked: Safari history "How to lock and hide chats", plus the deleted memo "Code woh hai… jo woh roz poochta tha", plus typing "pakki" in search. Players who got there called it the best puzzle in the game.
Fix: make hint 3 say "Type his secret code into WhatsApp's search. It's what Dilip asked him every day." Also make the dead WhatsApp tabs and chips either work or stop looking tappable. And reword the question so it doesn't quote the hidden line.

2. Episode 2 never ends by itself. Confirmed in code: ep2's end needs READY, which only the player's reply to Nitin sets.
Hit: Rhea ~15 commands, Arjun ~40, Tanvi STUCK-HARD 80+ (she waited, reloaded, tried airplane mode, put the case away), Kavya ~15, Priya ~15, Sahil, Ankit and Kabir.
Fix: after Q3 is filed, show Nitin's "Bhai? Online dikh raha hai" as a banner with a WhatsApp badge, and add a nudge after about 45 s ("Someone's online. Check WhatsApp.").

3. Filing proofs is opaque. Nearly every player hit this.
- One generic error, "Not filed. Either a word isn't what the phone shows, or the proof on the table doesn't show all of it.", for every kind of failure: wrong blank, missing proof, too many proofs. Dev ("me" failed 8 times, "Sameer Khurrana" passed) and Ankit (12 failures from stale pins off-screen) lost the most.
- Blanks and pinned proofs reset when you leave the case file to look for evidence (Arjun, Meher).
- The proof search box appends text and has no clear button (Kabir, Manav, Ankit).
- "Prove 3 things" filed with 2 (Kabir), because proofs are checked by facts covered, not by count, so the label can lie.
Fix: say which part failed ("the second blank" / "nothing here shows X" / "take one off"). Keep the case file's state while you hunt. Show "2 of 3 facts proven" instead of "Prove N things". Add a clear button to the search box.

4. A Detective hint points at the wrong proofs (Ep3 Q3). Confirmed. Hint 2 says "The badge on the waistcoat in the bag: you've seen it before, in Favorites…". That leads to a four-proof route (slip, badge, Anita, Nitin's 1:52), while hint 3 (help mode) gives a different three-proof set. Arjun spent ~60 commands on the Favorites tray photo and only filed after switching to help. Fix: make hint 2 lead to the same proofs as hint 3, or say "four things" outright.

Story holes and logic players raised

  • "Who am I?" is the biggest split. Most loved that every "Main Sameer nahi hoon" pays off. But Tanvi, Rohit, Ishita and Farhan felt they never had an honest way to say they were Sameer: every option denies it, so "held on 0 of 4" scores a forced choice. Bhasin's "Jiske paas bhi ye phone hai" and Meera's "Aap jo bhi ho" made them think they were a stranger. Consider: one honest option per call ("…") so the denials become a choice.
  • Why does Bhasin keep a shot witness alive for a week, and leave him his own phone? (Farhan, Zoya). The case file says "leverage". One line from Bhasin would settle it.
  • After the end: what happens to Bhasin, Kunal and "the DCP", who appears for the first time at the end (Kabir, Zoya)?
  • Why post the phone to an ex who blocked him? (Farhan). Meera's "Tu chahta tha koi tujhe pakde" answers it for those who reach Ep3.
  • No in-game police route. Tanvi and Neha wanted to dial 112 or get Raju to file a report. 112/14416 exist only as end-card links. Tanvi also couldn't tell Raju that Dilip was alive at 1:14 (the only option was "Pata nahi").
  • Never stated plainly: that Neelam ji is Mummy, that Bhasin is Kunal's "Papa", who owes the ₹1,80,000 (Sehgal Enterprises vs Bhasin Sehgal), and that lissy.anita is the nurse (Meher, Dev, Farhan). All can be inferred; each is one line.
  • Answered well (players called out the payoff): Dilip's chat "Read Tue 24 Nov, 3:12 AM" turns out to be the nurse. Bhasin's 3:31 "Body gadde mein…" comes after Dilip's 3:20 admission. Nitin's fever versus the car he sent. Dev's timeline audit found the night holds together.
