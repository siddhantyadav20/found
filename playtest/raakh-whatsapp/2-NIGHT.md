# The night: cue sheet

Run the night top to bottom. Each beat has a **trigger** (what has to happen first), **what you send** (copy-paste blocks, with media from `media/`), and the **Notes line** to post in 🗒 Notes.

- `📱` means send it in the **📱 The phone** chat. A name (`Raju`, `M`…) means send it in that character's chat.
- **A character's chat is created the first time they write** (see the [README](README.md#setup-about-30-minutes-once)). Its arrival is the notification. Each beat says when.
- `[file]` means attach that file from `media/` in the same spot.
- Lines marked **NEW** were written for this test. Everything else is from the current build.
- Every voice note goes with its words, written underneath it. Testers may play with the sound off.
- When the tester does something this sheet doesn't cover, stay in character and use [3-REACTIONS.md](3-REACTIONS.md).

**Two kinds of time, two markers.** `🕐` is only ever **tonight's clock**, and only ever posted in 📱. Notes lines about the night of the wedding carry their own date in bold: **Sun 22 Nov · 1:52 AM**. If a beat fires early and its `🕐` would be earlier than the last one you posted, skip that `🕐`: the clock never goes backwards.

**Every table is read top to bottom: use the first row that fits.**

**The rules for 🗒 Notes.** It records, it never concludes:
1. Post a line only **after the tester has reacted** to what it records (replied, said something, or moved on). Never while they're still reading or listening.
2. A line says **what an item shows, with its source**. Never what it means. No ✅, no "you did X".
3. Each ❓ is struck through when the story answers it, with nothing added.

**Playback picks.** From Beat 2 to Beat 7, whenever the tester says who they are or aren't, or promises someone something, copy their exact words and tonight's time (the last `🕐` you posted, plus the minutes since) into the *Playback picks* box on the [sheet](5-SHEET.md). You'll read three of them back at the reveal, so do it as it happens, not afterwards.
