# Found — Case 1: *Last Local*

*Design, 2026-10-18. **Built and playable** at `/c/lastlocal` (2026-10-18):
Siddhant asked for it built without waiting on approval, so the decisions at
the end were taken as recommended (see "Built", below).* Shagun stays as it
is; this replaces it as Case 1, and its end card opens Shagun as Case 2.

---

## The pitch

**1:12 AM. The last Virar fast, pulling out of Dadar.** A phone is wedged
into the seat beside you, face down, buzzing. You turn it over: twenty-three
missed calls from *Aai* (Mum), a live location moving, and one text on the
lock screen:

> **Unknown:** Jisne bhi ye phone uthaya hai — mere ghar walon ko kuch mat batana. Please.
> *Whoever picked up this phone: don't tell my family anything. Please.*

For the next hour the train runs north through Mumbai in real time:
Andheri, Borivali, Bhayander, Vasai, Nalasopara, Virar. Everyone looking for
the girl who owns this phone is looking for *the phone*. And it's in your
hand.

**One sentence:** a runaway's phone falls into your lap on the last train
home, and before Virar you must decide who she's really running from, and
who gets what's on it.

---

## Why this fixes what Shagun got wrong

| Shagun | Last Local |
|---|---|
| The crime was a week old and over before you began | Everything is happening **now**, and the train keeps moving |
| You couldn't change anything: Dilip was dead, Sameer escaped | Every reply changes **who finds whom**, and the ending is decided by what you do |
| You solved it by filling in sentences and ticking proof | You play **by using the phone**: reply, call, share or hide the location, forward, lie |
| The threat to you came late and resolved off-screen | From minute one the phone is a **beacon**: someone is following it, and you're carrying it |
| One chain, told backwards; every twist re-asked "how guilty is Sameer?" | Two clean turns that each **flip who you're helping**, and an ending you play |
| The climax was editing a post | The climax is **four stations against the clock**, with a man walking through the compartments toward you |

## The people

| | Who they seem to be | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Ananya Pawar**, 23 | A runaway daughter; family says she's "not well" and being "used by a man" | A junior structural engineer at her father's firm, carrying the proof that her father built the building that killed fourteen people, and forged her signature to blame her |
| **Sanjay Pawar** (*Papa*) | A frantic father | Owner of Pawar Infra; added two illegal floors to Shree Sai Heights, Nalasopara, against her rejected report |
| **Aai** (Mum) | Crying voice notes, "Beta ghar aa ja" (child, come home) | Wants her daughter home, but wants the phone more |
| **Rohit**, 27, her brother | Angry, hunting her ("Dhoond ke laaunga usko", I'll find her and bring her back) | The family's muscle, and the first person to call *you* |
| **Shinde Kaka**, 58 | "A family friend helping look" | A retired police inspector on Pawar's payroll, watching the phone's location from the family plan. The man walking toward you |
| **Imran Shaikh**, 31 | Everyone warns her off him: "Woh tujhe use kar raha hai" (he's using you), a police case against him | An independent journalist whose sister **Rubina** died in the collapse. The case is from a protest outside the building |

## What really happened (the truth the player uncovers)

- **Six weeks ago:** Shree Sai Heights, Nalasopara, collapses at 5 AM. Fourteen dead, Rubina Shaikh among them. The official story: tenants made illegal alterations.
- **Last week:** the state calls an inquiry, with a hearing at 10 AM tomorrow. In the firm's file, the structural approval carries **Ananya's signature**. She never signed it: she rejected the design, and her report is the one that's missing.
- **Tonight, 11:40 PM:** she finds her original rejected report in her father's study, photographs every page, and records him on a call: *"Do floor aur. Report main sambhaal lunga."* (Two more floors. I'll handle the report.) She leaves with a bag.
- **12:50 AM:** she messages Imran from Dadar. His paper's e-edition locks at **2:30 AM**. If he has the original photographs, with their metadata, before then, it runs in the morning, before the 10 AM hearing.
- **1:08 AM, the Virar fast at Dadar:** she sees Shinde on the platform. Her phone is the thing he can track, so she pushes it into the seat beside a stranger (you), steps off as the doors close, and borrows a phone to message her own.

**What the player must work out:**
1. The family isn't the victim; she is.
2. The phone matters more to them than she does.
3. **The final turn:** not every message from "Ananya" is from Ananya.

---

## How you play: through the phone, never through a form

- **There is no case file and no quiz.** Two things sit on the screen instead.
- **The line on the lock screen.** One plain sentence of what's going on, updated by the story, never a question:
  - *Someone is tracking this phone.*
  - *Ananya needs it before 2:30.*
- **The train bar.** A thin strip across the top: *Dadar → Andheri, 4 min*. The stations arrive on a clock, roughly in real time, so the deadline is felt rather than told.
- **Moves.** Every reply, call or toggle is a move, and moves have consequences you see later:
  - *Reply* as yourself, or *as her*.
  - *Answer* or decline calls.
  - *Location sharing* on or off. Turning it off alerts the family plan, and Shinde notices.
  - *Forward* the files. The upload crawls on train data, and you watch it.
  - *Call 139*, the railway helpline.
  - *Get off* at a station, or stay on.
- **Belief.** Three times the story asks you, inside a conversation, who you believe: Rohit asks *"Tum kis taraf ho?"* (Whose side are you on?), and Ananya asks *"Tumne kisko bataya?"* (Who did you tell?). What you've found decides which replies you can give. Nothing is graded; it changes what happens.
- **Kept simple:** one phone (hers), one of yours, a handful of apps (WhatsApp, Photos, Files, Find My Device, Phone), Hinglish with English under every line, and a five-person cast introduced one at a time.

## Episode 1 — *Dadar → Andheri* (about 15 min, 1:12–1:27 AM)

**Goal the player feels:** whose phone is this, and is she in danger?

1. **The buzz.** The phone vibrates in the seat. The lock screen shows 23 missed calls from *Aai*, a moving live-location card, and the "don't tell my family" text. You swipe up.
2. **Rohit calls within a minute.** He's warm and panicked: *"Aap kaun? Ye meri behen ka phone hai. Woh theek nahi hai, kisi ladke ke saath bhaagi hai. Please, aap kahan ho?"* (Who are you? This is my sister's phone. She isn't well, she ran off with some guy. Please, where are you?)
   - **First big move:** tell him the train, lie, or hang up.
   - Most players will tell him. It's the decent thing, and it's the wrong thing.
3. **What's in plain view:**
   - Aai's crying voice notes.
   - The family group "Pawar Parivar 🏠", full of worry.
   - Imran's last message: *"Platform 4. Mat rukna. Phone band kar."* (Don't stop. Turn your phone off.) Sinister out of context.
   - Her Instagram story from Dadar, 12:41 AM: a bag at her feet.
4. **The unknown number writes again** (it's really Ananya, on a borrowed phone):
   - If you told Rohit: *"Tumne bata diya na. …Ab woh tumhare peeche hain."* (You told them, didn't you. Now they're after you.)
   - If you didn't: *"Shukriya. Phone on rakhna, band mat karna."* (Thank you. Keep the phone on, don't switch it off.)
5. **Andheri.** A Find My Device notification: *"Shinde (Family) is viewing this device's location."* A car icon appears on the map, on the Western Express Highway, keeping pace with the train.

**Cliffhanger:** someone you've never heard of is following the phone, and the phone is in your hand.

---

## Episode 2 — *Andheri → Borivali* (about 20 min, 1:27–1:42 AM)

**Goal the player feels:** why does everyone want this phone?

1. **Photos (the investigation):**
   - Fourteen pages of a structural report stamped **REJECTED**, signed *A. Pawar*.
   - A second approval page with the same signature, but wrong. Zoom in and the loops differ: the forgery.
   - A news screenshot: *"Nalasopara collapse: 14 dead, tenants blamed."*
   - A photo of a girl in a school uniform, saved from Imran's chat: Rubina, sixteen.
2. **Voice Memos:** her father, 11:52 PM: *"Do floor aur. Report main sambhaal lunga."*
3. **Imran's chat, read properly:** his sister died in that building. *"Platform 4"* was the plan to meet; the police case is from the protest.
   - **First turn lands:** the family isn't saving her. They're burying her.
4. **Aai's mistake.** A voice note meant for the family group lands in Ananya's chat: *"Shinde ko bolo, phone mile toh ladki ko jaane de. Phone zaroori hai."* (Tell Shinde: if he gets the phone, let the girl go. The phone is what matters.)
5. **Big moves:**
   - **Forward the files to Imran now?** He's in a dead zone: one grey tick, a stalled upload.
   - **Turn off location?** The family plan pings Rohit, and Shinde calls *you*, quiet and polite: *"Beta, phone de do. Kisi ka nuksaan nahi hoga."* (Son, just give me the phone. Nobody will get hurt.)
   - **Answer Rohit** when he asks *"Tum kis taraf ho?"* (Whose side are you on?).
6. **Borivali.** The car icon stops at the station. A message in the family group: *"Shinde chacha train mein chadh gaye."* (Uncle Shinde has boarded the train.)

**Cliffhanger:** he's on this train.

---

## Episode 3 — *Borivali → Virar* (about 20 min, 1:42–2:14 AM): the climax

**Goal the player feels:** get the proof to the right person, and get off alive.

1. **Ananya, by text from the unknown number:** *"Vasai Road pe utarna. Platform 6. Main wahan hoon. 2:03."* (Get off at Vasai Road. Platform 6. I'm there. 2:03.)
2. **Shinde moves through the train.** Rohit's messages track him: *"Ladies ke baad wale dabbe mein hai… ab general mein…"* (He's in the compartment after the ladies'… now the general one…). Each update arrives closer. The train bar counts down Bhayander, then Vasai.
3. **The upload**, if started, creeps: 41%… 58%… and drops in the gap between Mira Road and Bhayander.
4. **The final turn (Bhayander, 1:55).** The phone rings. A payphone number. It's **Ananya's voice**: *"Maine tumhe Vasai ka koi message nahi bheja. Hum Virar pe hain."* (I never sent you any message about Vasai. We're at Virar.)
   - The "Vasai" texts were **Shinde**, on the phone he took from the girl who lent hers to Ananya.
   - **Fair-play clues for anyone reading closely:**
     - The real Ananya calls everyone *tu*; the Vasai messages say *aap*.
     - Her real plan, in Imran's chat, says *Virar*.
     - The Vasai number is the one Shinde's car was parked beside on the map.
5. **Vasai Road, 2:03.** The doors open. A timed choice, about 20 seconds:
   - **Get off** (into Shinde's arms).
   - **Stay on** (he's two doors down and saw you look).
   - **Throw the phone onto the platform** (a decoy), or **hand it to the woman beside you**.
   - **Call 139.**
6. **Nalasopara, 2:08.** The train passes the empty lot where Shree Sai Heights stood: one line, no music. If the upload is still running, it finishes here or it doesn't.
7. **Virar, 2:14.** Who's waiting on the platform depends on everything you did.

## Endings (decided by play, shown on both phones)

| Ending | How you get it | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning Edition** | Caught the impostor, stayed on to Virar, files reached Imran before 2:30 | Imran's headline lands at 2:29; Ananya hugs a stranger (you) on platform 1; at 10 AM, a notification: *"Pawar Infra owner detained"* |
| **The Decoy** | Threw the phone at Vasai to draw Shinde off, but the files were already sent | Shinde holds an empty phone; the story runs; Ananya texts you a photo of the sunrise from Virar |
| **Wrong Platform** | Got off at Vasai | Shinde takes the phone; at 10 AM the inquiry names the engineer who "signed" the approval: Ananya |
| **Lost and Found** | Called 139 and handed the phone in | The phone sits in a railway lost-property locker, logged; the hearing is adjourned; Rohit's lawyer collects "his sister's phone" at noon |
| **The Family's Version** | Told Rohit everything and never turned | Ananya is home by 3 AM; Aai's last voice note: *"Sab theek ho gaya beta."* (Everything's all right now, child.) The morning paper: *"Engineer's signature found on collapsed building's approval."* |

Each ending plays on your phone, as Shagun's morning now does, and ends on
one image:
- Rubina's school photo.
- Ananya at Virar.
- An empty platform at Vasai.

## Why it spreads

- **The first line:** *"A phone is buzzing in the seat next to you on the last train."* Every Mumbaikar has been on that train.
- **The share card:** *"I stayed on at Vasai."* / *"I got off at Vasai."* A one-line moral split people argue about.
- **Real time:** the Western line's stations arrive on a clock, so you play it on your own commute home.

---

## What it reuses, and what's new

- **Reused from Shagun's engine:**
  - the iPhone and its apps (WhatsApp, Photos, Voice Memos, Phone, Files)
  - your phone
  - calls, banners, timed events and the parcel-style opening
  - the morning-after endings
  - the sound pipeline
- **New:**
  - the train bar and a station clock
  - Find My Device with a moving map (her location, Shinde's car)
  - **moves** (location toggle, upload with progress, get off / stay on, call 139)
  - timed choices
  - belief replies gated on what you've found
  - endings computed from moves
  - removing the case file for this chapter
- **Assets:**
  - About 15 generated photos: the report pages, the forged approval, Rubina, the collapse site, Ananya's story frame, the platform at Vasai.
  - Train sound beds (CC0): a local at speed, doors, the station announcement chime.
  - The Marathi/Hindi announcements stay as captions until voices are recorded.

**Build size:** larger than any one earlier session. About three sessions:
1. The story as data, plus the train clock and moves.
2. Episodes 2–3, the climax and the endings.
3. Assets, a blind play-through and fixes.

## Built (2026-10-18)

- **Content:** `content/lastlocal/` (meta, story). Played end to end in the
  browser and by `tests/lastlocal.test.ts` (every ending, the resend path,
  location off, the train, the status slip, gender-neutral player lines).
- **Engine, new and shared:** the cold open with a bed (`coldOpen.bed`), the
  seat in place of the parcel (`story.seat`), timed moments
  (`components/stage/Moment.tsx`), endings decided by flags (`RESOLVE`),
  the route and the m-Indicator widget, the "Right now" slip
  (`status`, with `unless`), Find My (`components/owner/apps/FindMy.tsx`),
  chain links traced by `when`, word passwords and paper documents in Notes,
  the train under everything (`ambience: "train"`).
- **The decisions below, as taken:** the story as written; the stations run
  on their own clock (events with delays, a minute of story about a minute
  of play) with each episode's title card between; her phone is an iPhone.
- **Each case page now ships only its own story:** the server passes it to
  `CaseProvider`, which keeps the case page under its 700 KB budget.
- **Not done:** Rubina's photo is a captioned frame (image credits ran out,
  then Figma lost its connection); voices are captions.

## Decisions for Siddhant

1. **Approve the story as written**, or change any beat, cast member or ending.
2. **Near real time:** should the stations run on a real clock (about one minute per minute, with a pause between episodes), or on the player's pace?
3. **The phone:** hers is an iPhone (fast to build, reuses everything) or an Android, which fits a 23-year-old Mumbai engineer better but means building a second phone UI.
