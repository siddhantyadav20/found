# Low Battery — Shot List

Every photograph on the missing person's phone, re-made as real phone photos
of real Indian places.

The ones in `public/found/photos/` today read as fake, and for two reasons.
They're stock shots from cameras, not phones: shallow depth of field, tripod
long exposures, colour grading. And none of them is in India (a Vienna cinema
sign, a Japanese phone booth, a European mailbox bank). A filter can't fix
either, so every slot gets a new photograph.

**The split, decided 2026-09-15: Siddhant shoots what he can; Claude sources
openly licensed candidates for the rest, each approved before download.**

## For every shot

- **Your phone's main camera, default settings.** No filters, no Portrait mode
  (its fake blur is the stock look we're getting away from), and leave Night
  mode to do what it does.
- **Imperfect is right.** A bit of noise, a tilted horizon, motion blur, a
  flash that blows out the foreground. This is a 19-year-old's camera roll,
  not a portfolio.
- **No identifiable faces.** A real person's face must never play a suspect.
  Backs, hands and silhouettes are fine.
- **No real business as the subject.** A shop sign in the background is fine.
  A real mill, cinema or society name as the point of the photo is not: the
  story has an arson in it.
- **Mumbai, or anywhere that passes for it.** The story is set in Parel,
  Lalbaug and Andheri West.
- **Hand-over:** put them in `~/Desktop/Found photos/`, named by slot (e.g.
  `locker.jpg`). I'll strip the location data, size them, and blur any names
  on letterboxes or doors.

## Why these are yours to shoot

`locker`, `van`, `cake`, `story` and `letterbox` each carry something the
story needs: a code, a word, a gate, a hand-written flat number. No openly
licensed library will have them. `shoes` and `fuel` are easy to make and
hard to find.
